# Following the Grass

A VR game about choosing a seasonal camp on the Mongolian grassland. Players leave the family ger, explore with Bataa and decide where to settle the herd as the weather changes.

## 📖 Weekly Development Journal

| Week | Journal entry | What I worked on |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Initial research and reflection](week1.md) | Looked at how VR games use the player's hands and viewpoint to introduce unfamiliar actions. |
| 02 | [Choosing a direction](week2.md) | Compared ten project ideas and narrowed the focus to life on the Mongolian grassland. |
| 03 | [Research and early game direction](week3.md) | Used research on pastoral movement to frame the player's task of choosing a camp. |
| 04 | [Researching a living pasture](week4.md) | Studied how animals searching for food and shelter can give players clues about a landscape. |
| 05 | [Four seasons, one decision](week5.md) | Set out a prototype in which the same camp decision changes with pasture, water and weather. |
| 06 | [Modelling the ger and Bataa](week6.md) | Built the family's ger and developed Bataa's clothing and silhouette from visual references. |
| 07 | [Spring grassland blockout](week7.md) | Tested wind in grass mesh patches, widened the terrain and placed the river, gers and livestock. |
| 08 | [Landscape tuning and ger interior](week8.md) | Adjusted grass cover and water reflections, then tested furniture, roof light and the starting view inside the ger. |
| 09 | [The spring patrol route](week9.md) | Connected the guide, flock, ridge, river and horse so each stop gives the player a reason to move on. |
| 10 | [Dialogue, subtitles and lip sync](week10.md) | Paired Bataa's spoken lines with subtitles and tested how his mouth moved during conversation. |
| 11 | [The grassland soundscape](week11.md) | Tuned wind, grass and water so the walk from the ger to the river sounds different at each stop. |
| 12 | [Playing through spring](week12.md) | Joined dialogue, petting a sheep, riding and returning to camp into one playable sequence. |
| 13 | [Interface design](week13.md) | Designed the field guide, map pins, ger selector and report that carry an observation through to a decision. |
| 14 | [The campsite scoring code](week14.md) | Sampled terrain and vegetation, weighted the results by ger type and translated them into feedback. |
| 15 | [Summer rain](week15.md) | Compared a grassy hollow with raised ground to show how rain changes drainage and site scores. |
| 16 | [Autumn wind](week16.md) | Used gusts, moving grass and sheep distribution to help players compare an exposed ridge with sheltered pasture. |
| 17 | [Winter snow](week17.md) | Added snow depth, exposed forage and open water as clues to where the herd can graze and drink. |
| 18 | [Cover and chapter selection](week18.md) | Refined the title screen and chapter selector so players can preview the weather before entering a level. |
| 19 | [Comparing all four seasons](week19.md) | Traced grass, slope, livestock and water through the scoring code to explain why a site's value changes across seasons. |
